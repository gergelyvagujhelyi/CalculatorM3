Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Calculator M3

Calculator M3 is a free, open-source calculator app built by Gergely Vagujhelyi.

Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All calculations and history are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

AI Math Solver (optional)

The app includes an optional AI Math Solver. You can photograph a handwritten math expression — using your device's system camera, or by picking an existing photo — write it on screen, or type it, and a locally-stored AI model reads and solves it entirely on your device. Nothing you photograph, write or type is ever uploaded. If you do not use this feature, it has no effect.

AI history. Each solved problem is kept in the app's AI history, on your device only, so you can look at it again: what you typed or drew, or a reduced copy of the photo (at most 768 pixels on its longest side; your original photo is not kept), together with the model's answer. It is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is not included in cloud backups; like your calculation history, it moves to a new phone if you transfer your data to it. The newest 200 entries are kept. You can delete any entry, or clear the whole AI history, from the History screen; uninstalling the app also removes it.

Internet Access

The calculator works entirely offline and makes no network connections. The only feature that uses the internet is the optional AI Math Solver: when you choose to download an AI model, the app downloads it from a public model host (such as Hugging Face). The model then runs entirely on your device. If you never download an AI model, the app uses no internet at all.

If you choose to have AI answers in your own language, the app also downloads that language's translation model once (15–38 MB), from Mozilla's public model storage (Firefox Translations). The translation then runs entirely on your device too: the answers themselves are never sent anywhere.

Some models are "gated" and require a one-time sign-in to Hugging Face, which you start yourself. The sign-in happens directly with Hugging Face in a browser tab — your credentials are entered on Hugging Face's own page and are never seen by this app. The app stores only a short-lived access token on your device, used solely to authorize that download. The token is excluded from device backups and is not copied to a new phone when you transfer your data.

Third-Party Services

This app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. AI models are downloaded from public hosts (such as Hugging Face, and Mozilla's storage for translation models) solely to run on your device; no usage data is shared with them. If you choose to download a gated model, you sign in to Hugging Face directly, and the app receives only a short-lived download token — never your credentials.

Permissions

The calculator itself requests no special permissions. The optional AI Math Solver uses:

  • Internet and network state — to download an AI model you choose, and the translation model if you choose answers in your language (one-time).
  • Foreground service and notifications — to show download progress and keep a large model download running reliably.

Photos are captured through your device's system camera app, so the app needs no camera permission of its own.
